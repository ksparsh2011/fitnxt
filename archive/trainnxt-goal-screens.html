TrainNXT — Goal Tracking

The missing screens: Onboarding intake, Goal visibility on dashboard, Body metrics log, BMI update flow, and Goal detail page.

What was missing from v1: The user had no way to set their goal, no way to see progress toward it on the dashboard, no BMI history log, no way to log a new BMI scan, and no alerts for when to get re-tested. These 5 screens fix all of that. The goal card from Screen B now appears at the top of every dashboard view.

📐 Design decisions explained
Why goal card on dashboard
The goal is the reason the user opens the app. It should be the first thing they see after greeting — not buried in a progress tab. Tapping it goes to Screen E. The card shows current → target at a glance, no tap required.
Why estimated body fat
Between DEXA scans, the system estimates body fat using body weight trend + session volume data. It's clearly labelled "est." — never presented as fact. Gives the user meaningful data without requiring monthly clinic visits.
BMI scan alert logic
Alert fires at day 39 (1 week before 6-week mark). Science-based: 6 weeks is the minimum meaningful window for body composition change on any test. Alert explains why — not just "time to test" but "here's the reason."
Photo scan reading
Claude Vision reads DEXA and InBody printout photos. Most clinics give you a printed report. User snaps a photo, Claude extracts all the numbers automatically. Removes manual entry friction completely.
AI live analysis on entry
When user enters new scan data (Screen D), Claude analyses the delta in real-time before they even save. If progress is ahead of schedule, Claude tightens the target. If behind, it adjusts the calorie deficit and plan. The plan is never static.
Goal types beyond body fat
The same system works for: strength goals (squat 100kg 1RM — tracked via PR table), weight goals (reach 75kg), endurance goals (run 5km — tracked via cardio logs), recomp (gain lean mass while losing fat — both tracked). Onboarding screen A routes to the right tracking type.
