🎨 Design System — TrainNXT
Color Palette
Lime
Ind
Amb
BG
Surf
Electric lime = progress, energy, positive action. Indigo = AI. Amber = PRs and achievements. Dark navy base.
Typography
Syne — Display, headings, numbers. Geometric, bold, athletic.

DM Sans — Body text. Clean, readable in gym lighting.

JetBrains Mono — Data: weights, reps, times. Code-like precision.
Key UX Decisions
Touch targets min 48px — sweaty gym gloves
Bottom navigation — one thumb, no reach
Optimistic UI — sets log instantly
Mono font for data — scan faster
Dark theme — gym lighting, OLED battery
On HLD/LLD diagrams
The C4 ASCII diagrams in the docs are architecturally correct but not industry presentation-grade. For proper HLD use Draw.io or Miro. For LLD/sequence diagrams use PlantUML. For Figma — this prototype IS your Figma substitute until the app is built.
